FAQ

Can I get a second opinion online?

It depends on your case and the hospital, but online consultations are possible in some cases. Please tell us your situation first.

What medical records do I need?

A medical report, test results, and CT/MRI images help things go smoothly. Please reach out even if you don't have everything.

How much does it cost?

Costs depend on your case, the format, and the hospital. As a guide:

  • Easy-to-start options such as an online second opinion: from around ¥100,000
  • We also offer full-support plans that cover an in-person visit to Japan, an interpreter at your side, and transfers

We always confirm the final cost before anything starts, and proceed only after you agree.

Can you guarantee treatment in Japan?

Whether treatment is possible is decided by the hospital, so we cannot guarantee it. We start by checking the possibilities and options with you.

How long does it take to hear back?

Once your records are ready, we can usually tell you whether consultation is possible within about 1–2 weeks (this varies by hospital).

How do I get started?

Send us a short note on WhatsApp about your symptoms or diagnosis. You can share medical records later.

About us

We combine medicine and technology to make healthcare accessible across borders. By connecting people around the world with high-quality medical care in Japan, we help them get care beyond the barriers of language and culture. Through our network of medical institutions and our digital tools, we work toward a future where anyone can choose the care that is right for them, with peace of mind.

Team

Our team in Japan handles communication with hospitals, organizes your records, and coordinates your schedule. You can consult us in English or Indonesian.

A message from our CEO
